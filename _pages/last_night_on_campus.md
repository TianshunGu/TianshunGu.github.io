I wanted to remember my last night on campus in China this year. Before leaving for my time abroad, I hoped to see as many friends and family members as I could. I’m beginning to admit that the older I get, the less of my time feels like my own. Still, I was glad for the time I did have.

I was glad I got to go home and eat my parents’ homemade dumplings with my little brother.  
I was glad Jiaojiao and I got to feed the stray cats outside her building, and that I got to pose as a visiting teacher and watch her make a difficult lesson look easy.  
I was glad I got to see my grandmother at my uncle’s place after far too long.  
I was glad I got to take my senior labmates in the PhD program, who are graduating next year, out for an early farewell dinner.  
I was glad our little dog had grown even more attached to me.  
I was glad I finally got to see what it was like to sleep in while Jiaojiao went to work.  
I was glad…

The list has to end somewhere. I could linger over every goodbye, but I want to remember why I chose to set out in the first place, and why I was willing to go so far from home.

I hope I can shape the years ahead the way I’ve imagined.  
I hope that when I come back next year, I’ll be glad I made this choice.  
I hope the rest of the journey goes smoothly, and that all will be well.
